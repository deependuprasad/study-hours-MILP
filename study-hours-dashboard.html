How few hours does it take to hit your target CGPA?

A mixed integer linear programme that splits a student's study time across five MBA courses. It finds the smallest number of hours that still reaches a target CGPA under relative grading.

01 Problem Statement

Study time is scarce, and grading is relative

How should a student split limited study hours across five courses so that they reach a target CGPA with the fewest total hours?
5
courses this term, 6 credits each
4 bands
grade set by position in the class (10 / 9 / 8 / 7)
70 / 100
marks that respond to study (mid-sem + final)
↓ returns
each extra hour earns fewer marks than the last

Relative grading: grades follow the class curve

Last year's class mean μ and standard deviation σ split the marks into four bands. Beating μ + σ earns a 10. The same marks can earn a different grade in a different course.

Where the 100 marks come from

Only the two exams depend on individual study hours. The project and attendance are fixed inputs.

Trade-off. Hours spent on one course can't go to another. Confidence differs by course, so the same hour buys more marks in some courses than in others. The model decides which grade to target in each course, and how many hours that grade needs.

Width w and slope β: diminishing returns, like electricity slabs in reverse

The first w₁ = 10 hours earn β₁ = 2.0 marks per hour. The next 10 earn 1.2, and the last 15 earn 0.6. Confidence scales every slope by η. Because β₁ > β₂ > β₃, the solver fills segment 1 first on its own, so no extra binary variables are needed.

Example (η = 1, 15 h): 10 h × 2.0 + 5 h × 1.2 = 26 exam marks → h₁ = 10, h₂ = 5, h₃ = 0.

03 Objective Function

Minimise total study hours

The target CGPA is a requirement, not the goal. Once it's met, every hour saved can go to placements, clubs or rest.

min  Z = Σi=15 ti = Σi ( hi1 + hi2 + hi3 )Excel: Set Objective → Model!$B$38 = SUM(Hours t), To: Min

Why minimise hours?

Study time has an opportunity cost. A plan that reaches 9.0 in 45 h beats one that reaches it in 80 h, because the 35 spare hours have other uses.

Why is it linear?

Z is a plain sum of hour variables, and every marks and grade relationship is a linear equation or inequality. The step from marks to grade is handled with binary variables y, which makes the model a mixed integer LP.

The mirror question

Dropping the CGPA constraint and maximising Σ cixi / Σ ci within Tmax gives the best CGPA reachable. Solving both shows whether the target is achievable and at what cost.

04 Constraints

Nine constraint families keep the plan feasible and realistic

Each one maps to a constraint row in Excel Solver.

Implementation note: degeneracy. When the hour floor tmin forces more hours than a grade needs, many splits of h are equally optimal. That's why Excel can show h₃ filling before h₁. Total hours and grades are unaffected. This dashboard breaks the tie by filling segments in order, and a tiny penalty on h₂ and h₃ does the same in Excel.
